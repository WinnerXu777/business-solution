# SMB ERP and CRM Competitive Landscape and Pricing (US/Canada, 5–200 employees), as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-24. Official pricing pages were fetched on that date unless another date is given. All prices are USD unless marked CAD. "Est." means a third-party estimate, not a vendor list price. Fetched pages were summarised by a fetch tool, so on a few pages (Odoo, GoHighLevel) the monthly vs annual columns were garbled. Those cases are flagged.

## Q1. 2025–2026 list prices and pricing models (per user, per resource, onboarding fees, minimum contracts)

### Takeaway
CRM pricing is public and per seat: about $7–$40/seat at the low end (Zoho, Pipedrive, Freshsales, monday, HubSpot Starter) and $90–$195/seat at the mid tier (HubSpot Pro, Salesforce Pro/Core, Close Growth). HubSpot Pro/Enterprise also charge required onboarding fees. ERP pricing splits into two groups. Business Central, Odoo, Zoho and QBO publish prices (roughly $25–$110/user/mo). NetSuite, Acumatica, Sage Intacct, SAP B1, Intuit Enterprise Suite and Katana Advantage are quote-only, and those quotes usually start at about $6K–$12K/yr in platform fees before users. GoHighLevel stands apart: it charges a flat agency fee with unlimited users or sub-accounts.

### Cited Findings

#### ERP

**Oracle NetSuite (quote-only; all figures are third-party estimates)**
- Est. base platform fee about $999/mo (some sources give $999–$2,000/mo). Est. full users $99–$199/user/mo ($1,200–$2,400/user/yr). Modules (CRM, ecommerce, manufacturing, WMS) are priced separately. 3-year contracts typically get 20–30% off list. Several guides warn that prices often rise at renewal. — [WebSearch summary of BPM, Numeric, Redress Compliance, Stacksync guides](https://www.bpm.com/insights/netsuite-licensing-pricing/); [Redress Compliance](https://redresscompliance.com/oracle-netsuite-pricing-guide.html); [Numeric](https://www.numeric.io/blog/netsuite-price)
- Rand Group (published May 13 2026, updated Jul 29 2026): pricing "is not fixed and is not publicly listed". SuiteSuccess comes in Standard and Premium editions per industry. The Standard edition is capped at 30 general-access users, and Premium is required above that. Service tiers (Standard/Premium/Enterprise/Ultimate) govern capacity, and the Standard service tier allows at most 100 users. Initial-term discounts do not necessarily carry into later terms. — [Rand Group](https://www.randgroup.com/insights/oracle-netsuite/how-much-does-netsuite-cost/)

**Acumatica (quote-only; pricing is based on resources and transactions, not users)**

Add-on modules (Advanced Financials, CRM, Project Accounting, Payroll) raise the price. 50GB of storage is included. — [Cargas](https://cargas.com/software/acumatica/pricing/)
- Other est.: cloud subscriptions start around $6,000/yr for small organisations. One source (Feb 2025) estimates $25K–$50K/yr plus $50K–$100K implementation for a company with 5–20 employees. That conflicts with the Cargas figures. — [WebSearch summary incl. Stacksync, Costbench, ERP Research](https://www.stacksync.com/blog/acumatica-pricing-guide); [Costbench](https://www.costbench.com/software/erp/acumatica/)

### Inferences
- The model breaks down into three types:
  1. Per seat: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Freshsales, monday, Close, BC, Odoo, SAP B1
  2. Per resource or transaction with unlimited users: Acumatica, Katana Advantage, Frappe Cloud (compute)
  3. Flat platform fee plus per-user fee: NetSuite base + users, Sage Intacct, Intuit Enterprise Suite; GoHighLevel is flat per agency
  
  Unlimited-user and flat models are the main differentiators against per-seat incumbents.
- The quote-only vendors (NetSuite, Acumatica, Intacct, SAP B1) all start at roughly $6K–$20K/yr before implementation. That sets a high floor for a 10-person company.

## Q2. Typical implementation costs and timelines for SMBs, and real cost for a 10-user and a 25-user company

### Takeaway
Implementation is usually the largest year-1 cost for ERP: roughly 1–2.5x the annual subscription. Typical SMB projects cost $15K–$50K (Business Central, Odoo, Acumatica at the small end), $25K–$150K+ (NetSuite) or $2K–$12K (ERPNext with a lean partner). Timelines run 6–14 weeks. CRMs mostly need little implementation apart from HubSpot's required $1,500/$3,500 onboarding.

## Q3. AI and agent features and how they are priced

### Takeaway
Three AI monetisation models have emerged:
1. **Metered credits or outcomes**: HubSpot $0.50 per resolved conversation; Salesforce $0.10 per action or $2 per conversation; Freshsales $49 per 100 sessions; Close, monday and Zoho credit wallets.
2. **Bundled at no extra cost**: NetSuite (core AI and SuiteAgent), and Zoho Zia Agents (free to build, pay only model usage).
3. **Tier-gated**: Odoo Agentic AI only in the Custom plan; Salesforce Agentforce heavily in Max ($550) or add-ons from $150/user.

## Q4. Partner, reseller and implementer programmes and margins

### Takeaway
Licence margins are modest for open and low-cost platforms: Odoo 10–20% with a cap of $180 per subscription per month, plus 50% on Odoo.sh. They are larger for quote-based ERP: NetSuite says up to 50% on new licences but requires a $100K/yr commitment in North America; Acumatica claims "generous" tiered margins but does not publish them. In all these ecosystems the real money is services: $15K–$80K implementations plus $500–$3,000/mo support. GoHighLevel is the one platform where the reseller sets the price and keeps the whole spread. That makes it the clearest white-label precedent.

### Cited Findings

**Odoo**
- Recurring commission on Enterprise licences: **Ready 10%, Silver 15%, Gold 20%** (Learning tier 10%). Odoo.sh hosting commission is 50% for Ready and above. ### Inferences
- **Economics of selling Odoo as a partner** (10-user client, Standard at about $31 × 10 = $311/mo): licence commission is about $31–$62/mo, well under the cap. A $15K–$35K implementation plus $500–$1,500/mo support dwarfs that. Partner revenue is roughly 90% services.
- **GoHighLevel template**: flat wholesale cost, white-label, reseller-set retail price, and marked-up usage (including AI). - **NetSuite's $100K/yr commitment** shuts out small new entrants. Odoo (low bar, capped commission) and Frappe (no licence fee) are the easiest ecosystems for a newcomer.

## Q5. Where is the price/value gap a new bundled vendor could exploit?

### Takeaway
The gap sits in the 10–50 user band. Today buyers choose between cheap but fragmented tools (QBO $340/mo, a CRM at $14–$40/seat, an inventory app at $229–$729/mo) and "real" ERP+CRM stacks that cost $60K–$150K in year 1. Most of that year-1 cost is implementation, and AI is increasingly metered per outcome or per credit. A vendor offering a flat or unlimited-user bundle with AI included and fixed-price, fast onboarding would undercut the per-seat, per-credit incumbents on the line items customers resent most.

### Cited Findings
- **Per-seat cost cliffs**:
  - HubSpot jumps from $20 to $90–$100/seat plus $1,500 onboarding between Starter and Pro. — [HubSpot Sales pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)
  - Salesforce jumps from $25 to $100 to $195/user between Starter, Pro and Core. — [Salesforce pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/)
- **Price-increase resentment**:
  - QBO Advanced +70% to $340/mo from Aug 2026. — [Certum](https://www.certumsolutions.com/library/quickbooks-price-increase-august-2026)
  - Business Central +$10/user from Oct/Nov 2025. — [Microsoft blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/blog/business-leader/2025/05/06/new-microsoft-dynamics-365-business-central-pricing-effective-november-2025/)
  - Salesforce +6% (Aug 2025) and Core at $195. — [SalesforceBen](https://www.salesforceben.com/salesforce-announces-6-pricing-increase-and-unlimited-agentforce-licenses/); [CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/4218804/salesforce-offers-more-agentforce-credits-to-drive-adoption.html)
  - Odoo Custom reportedly up sharply in Oct 2026. — [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/odoo/pricing.md)
  - HubSpot seat-migration complaints. — [HubSpot Community](https://community.hubspot.com/t/seat-based-pricing-model/119123)
- **Implementation dominates TCO**: 1–2x the subscription for NetSuite, 1.5–2.5x for Acumatica, $15K–$80K for Odoo at 10–25 users. — [Rand Group](https://www.randgroup.com/insights/oracle-netsuite/how-much-does-netsuite-cost/); [Cargas](https://cargas.com/software/acumatica/pricing/); [Octura](https://octurasolutions.com/resources/odoo-pricing-2026-complete-transparent-breakdown-us-canada)
- **AI is metered** at $0.10–$2 per action or conversation (Salesforce), $0.50 per resolution and $1 per lead (HubSpot), $49 per 100 sessions (Freshsales). — [Agentforce pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/); [HubSpot news](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspots-customer-agent-and-prospecting-agent-now-you-pay-when-the-task-is-complete); [Freshworks](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/)
- **Counter-trend**: NetSuite bundles AI at no extra charge; Zoho makes agents free and passes model costs through at about cost (1,000 credits per $1). A "free AI" pitch alone will not differentiate against them. — [Computer Weekly](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366622379/NetSuite-bets-big-on-built-in-AI); [Zoho Agents pricing](https://www.zoho.com/agents/pricing.html)
- **Unlimited-user models already exist** and are a known selling point: Acumatica (unlimited users with transaction caps), Katana (unlimited users), GoHighLevel (flat agency price). — [Cargas](https://cargas.com/software/acumatica/pricing/); [Katana](https://katanamrp.com/pricing/); [HighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing)
- **Low-cost bundles exist but need partners**: Zoho One at $37/user (first year only, all employees) and Odoo Standard at about $31. Odoo Community lacks US/Canada payroll and support. ERPNext reviews cite weak support and localisation. — [Zoho One](https://www.zoho.com/one/pricing.html); [Octura](https://octurasolutions.com/resources/odoo-pricing-2026-complete-transparent-breakdown-us-canada); [GetApp ERPNext](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/erpnext/)

### Inferences
- **Target price point**: a 25-user company spends roughly $30K–$60K/yr on licences for BC or NetSuite plus HubSpot or Salesforce Pro. Odoo or Zoho One costs about $9K–$27K/yr. A bundle at about $40–$60/user/mo all-in ($12K–$18K/yr for 25 users) would sit between the two:
  - Value of the bundle: ERP + CRM + AI agents included up to a fair-use cap, plus fixed-fee onboarding of about $5K–$15K
  - Undercut: roughly 50–70% below "proper" stacks
  - Edge over Odoo/Zoho: support, US/Canada payroll and tax localisation, and done-for-you setup
- **Fastest go-to-market**: build on ERPNext/Frappe ($0 licence, $5–$40/mo hosting, no revenue share) or become an Odoo partner (10–20% capped commission, services-led). Wrap it in GoHighLevel-style white-label SaaS mode so MSPs, bookkeepers and agencies resell at their own price with marked-up usage.
- **AI as a flat-included feature**, rather than credits, is a differentiator against HubSpot, Salesforce, Freshworks and monday. It is not one against NetSuite or Zoho, so the pitch should lean on outcomes (agents that do bookkeeping, collections, quoting) rather than on "AI included".
- **Canada angle**: Odoo prices CAD separately, and Canadian payroll and tax localisation is a known weakness for Odoo Community and ERPNext. A CAD-priced bundle with native Canadian payroll could be a niche wedge. This needs validation.

### Gaps
- No direct survey data on SMB willingness to pay for a bundled ERP+CRM+AI product.
- No sourced figures on churn or switching rates between these products.
- No sourced data on reseller or MSP demand for a white-label ERP (as opposed to the GoHighLevel marketing-CRM precedent).
