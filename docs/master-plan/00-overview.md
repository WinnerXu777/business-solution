# AI business platform master plan

## Executive summary

Build one subscription platform that helps small and midsize businesses run everyday operations and put AI to work under the same permissions and oversight as their staff. Launch in Toronto, Ontario, with industrial supplies distributors as the first sales segment. Plan the United States as the second market. Ontario customers get a Canadian hosting default and Canadian-dollar pricing from the start.

**What customers can buy.** The full product includes a small ERP and CRM for customers, orders, purchasing and inventory, connections to software customers already use, inbound chat and voice, a bot that drafts purchase orders, and a collections bot for email plus outbound AI voice and SMS behind a separate compliance gate. Staff can build workflows such as a campaign to win back inactive customers. The September 2027 Toronto release offers rules-based fraud review, statistical forecasts and customer profiles grounded in retrieval and prompting. Trained and fine-tuned versions are built in parallel and enabled per tenant after their data and evaluation gates pass.

**How customers make it their own.** Every service has a native option included in its subscription allowance. Customers can keep the native CRM, connect an existing accounting system, keep their contact centre or choose a premium voice or language model provider. They can mix choices by service. The platform shows where data goes and how each choice is billed. Premium services use a customer-held account, a disclosed pass-through charge or a quoted uplift.

**How customers stay in control.** Staff can see the business record behind an action, approve sensitive actions and pause a bot or workflow. The platform checks who may act, checks again just before a consequential action, and records what happened. This applies equally to built-in tools, customer workflows and outside apps. Ontario collections, Canadian privacy, channel consent and tax-record access are built into delivery from day one. Quebec has its own review before rollout.

**How we build it.** All workstreams start together, planned for January 2027 after funding, staffing and employment/IP clearance. Use self-hosted LangGraph. The founder works 15–25 hours a week on evenings and weekends while building a government AI platform full-time. The lead hire and contractors run delivery. The recommended schedule targets a September Toronto core release and broader availability through March 2028. The fully staffed alternative targets the wider catalog in September 2027, with outbound voice and SMS released only when separately cleared. December brings an assessment of US readiness. [Roadmap](03-roadmap.md)

**What it costs and how we measure it.** The recommended plan budgets **CAD $745,320** in 2027 cash spending including labour, with **$42,500** in expected receipts and **$702,820** in net cash needed. Secure **$800,000** before committing. The September full-catalog plan budgets **$1,006,100** gross, **$98,000** in expected receipts and **$908,100** net. Track staff time, service resolution, purchasing accuracy, review and forecast quality, onboarding effort and recurring margin. ## Market and product boundaries

Toronto and the surrounding GTA are the first sales and implementation territory. Focus on industrial supplies wholesalers with roughly 20–75 employees and one operating entity. Sell to a finance or operations lead and bring service, purchasing and account teams into onboarding. Use owner-led outreach alongside bookkeepers, CPAs and fractional CFOs. Commercial subcontractors and job shops are candidates for adjacent templates after Ontario delivery is repeatable. Select the first US state using customer pull, partner coverage and counsel review. Quebec requires its own Law 25 assessment before activation. [Market research](../../research_notes/SMB%20ERP%20CRM%20AI%20bot%20bundle/market_and_niches.md)

The research's estimate of about 650,000 US and Canadian firms with 20–199 employees covers many industries. Use local introductions, qualified opportunities, paid onboarding and renewals to validate the reachable market and willingness to pay. [Owner's market report](../../reports/SMB%20ERP%20CRM%20AI%20bot%20bundle.md)

Start with one reference operating template and let customers choose modules from the full catalog. Declare an authoritative system for each record type. The native ERP covers operational records and can hand reconciled data to the customer's accounting ledger. Product boundaries exclude a general ledger, payroll, a tax engine, funds custody, consumer collections, healthcare data, automated credit or employment decisions, and arbitrary code in customer workflows. [Module scope](02-modules.md)

## What to manage from day one

- **Parallel staffing.** The founder owns material architecture and customer decisions. The delivery lead coordinates daytime execution and assigns each workstream an implementer and reviewer. A designated second operator exercises recovery and incident procedures before live service.
- **Customer data.** Start imports, permissions, outcome labeling and model evaluation in January. Release explainable rules, statistical forecasts and evidence-based profiling in September. Use the same paths as fallbacks after tenant-specific trained versions pass their release checks.
- **External effects and providers.** Rehearse duplicate events, interruptions, outages and mismatched data. Verify provider locations and contract terms before enabling each route and isolate affected operations during an incident.
- **Delivery economics.** Measure booked implementation and support time, provider costs and margins by tenant. Standardize common mappings and use scoped setup quotes for nonstandard migrations.
