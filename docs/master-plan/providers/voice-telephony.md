# Voice, telephony and contact-centre providers

**Live web research and pricing snapshot — 2026-11-24.** Scope is a Toronto-first SMB platform expanding to the US, with a platform-operated default and service-level bring-your-own (BYO) credentials. Prices are vendor list snapshots, generally USD unless noted or currency is unclear. Every table's price column uses the snapshot date. Obtain local quotes for commitments, minimums, tax, carrier surcharges and add-ons. **Unverified** means the linked public material did not establish the claim for that exact product or plan.

**Read the evidence by workload.** Canadian DIDs, edge points, processing, storage, backups, logs, support access and subprocessors are distinct. PIPEDA is an accountability obligation across rows. Canadian privacy fit remains unverified for each actual deployment until the tenant's purpose, contract, data flows and controls are reviewed. The OPC discusses cross-border processing under PIPEDA, and Ontario health deployments require a separate PHIPA assessment. A vendor SOC 2 claim needs a current report and in-scope service. HIPAA eligibility requires the applicable configuration and signed business associate agreement (BAA) across the PHI path. Provider links are primary evidence unless identified otherwise. [OPC cross-border guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/airports-and-borders/gl_dab_090127/) · [PIPEDA requirements](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) · [Ontario IPC health privacy](https://www.ipc.on.ca/en/health-individuals)

**Production evaluation.** The quality column gives product strengths and evaluation priorities. Vendor timings are labelled and are estimates for their own measurement boundaries. Use the same Toronto PSTN and WebRTC test set for all providers, with en-CA, fr-CA, code-switching, interruptions, hold music, accents, noise, 8 kHz calls and transfers. Compare p50/p95 end-of-speech to first audible response, barge-in cut-off, call setup time, packet loss, transfer success, speech recognition errors on names and numbers, pronunciation, task completion and cost per resolved contact. Break down carrier, model, transport and storage costs. MCP (Model Context Protocol) entries identify public tool support where established. Keep media and call control on their documented protocols and scope side-effecting tools to tenant and session.
